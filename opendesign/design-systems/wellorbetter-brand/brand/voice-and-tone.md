# Voice and tone

The existing copy already had the right voice. This documents it so it does
not drift, and so the English and Chinese stay the same person.

## The voice in one line

Someone who builds tools for themselves, tells you plainly what works and
what doesn't, and doesn't oversell.

## Held rules

**Understate. Never hype.** The site says 「不是 demo 墙，是我真的在用的东西。」
It does not say "revolutionary" or "powerful". If something is Alpha, the
status pill says Alpha.

**Name the concrete problem first.** Every project description opens with
what it does, not why it matters. 「记录应用活跃时长、日历与回顾，所有数据只留在本机。」

**Admit the messy order.** 「先做成，再做对，再做好看。」 The process section is
honest that polish comes last. Keep that — it is the most distinctive thing
on the page.

**No filler sections.** No testimonials, no fake metrics, no "trusted by".
There are six projects because there are six worth showing.

## Casing and punctuation

- Chinese body copy uses full-width punctuation：，。、「」
- Latin eyebrows are uppercase (`VIBE CODING LAB · 2026`, `RECENT SHIPS`,
  `HOW I BUILD`). Chinese eyebrows are **not** — see the `:lang(zh)` rule.
- Project names keep their real casing: `cxs`, `TimeTrace`, `Window Stats`.
  Never title-case a lowercase tool name.
- Numbered process steps are zero-padded mono: `01`, `02`, `03`, `04`.
- No emoji. Not in copy, not as icons.

## Chinese ↔ English

Both locales exist and must read as written, not translated. Compare:

> zh: 把脑子里的小想法，做成真的能用的工具。
> en: Small ideas. Real tools. Shipped.

The English is shorter and more clipped — that is correct. Do not stretch
the English to match the Chinese sentence shape, or vice versa.

## Talking about itself vs. to the user

The site talks about the work in first person (「我真的在用的东西」) and never
addresses the visitor as a customer. There is no "you'll love", no
"get started today". The primary CTA is 「看最近作品」 — an invitation to look,
not to convert.
